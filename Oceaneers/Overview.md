Oceaneers is a game of educational entertainment!

My initial thoughts is to have the player start by flying via helicopter to a research facility. 

Perhaps contact with the station was lost and you are going there to investigate, possible food for thought to string together a deeper mystery.

The player would arrive, explore, investigate and begin problem solving to bring the station back online once more.

Eventually they would venture out into the ocean discovering the wonders of the sea. The creatures that reside there, the eco systems that it comprises of and their own impact upon it.

Research Facility as a static 'Main Base'.

Nautilus-style self-sustainable submarine {Modelled after a whale} as you exploratory vessel to visit various locations on the World Map.

Mini-Sub which may be launched from the 'Nautilus' that will allow the player to venture into sea caves, trenches etc.

This can also be an episodic adventure series to keep things manageable. 

Great potential for Mental Health / Mindfulness applications.
