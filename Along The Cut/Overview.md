Episodic adventures <-- Keeps workload manageable

The player has inherited the narrowboat from their Aunt Eira Elm who has been missing for over a year and now presumed dead and so her estate has passed to you. The player will begin to unravel the mystery of the 'Blight' whilst travelling the land of Romaine and its waterways, meeting a variety of interesting characters and uncovering the truth behind their Aunt's disappearance.
