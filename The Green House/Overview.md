This is an extraction-deckbuilding game centred around herbalism.

I am still trying to piece this together in my head but this is what I have come up with thus far:

The player finds themselves in the town of Schneeze Wharf {placeholder} how and why I have not really figured that out yet. It will have something to do with The Green House which is the herbalism shop they will manage.

The player's central hub is the town and there are various services available from a host of vendors and townsfolk that reside within it. These may also be quest givers as things progress.

Core Loop --> Forage, Survive, Heal

Starting Decks: Ranger | Pacifist | Druid

Cards are sorted in three types: Force, Focus, Finesse.

Herbal Cards have a decay timer applied to them. Ideally the player will make it back to the hub before their harvest becomes useless. It is worth noting that some decayed cards can have effects of their own when combined with others.

There is a database of herbs that I created in Notion a couple years back from real-world texts. It is verbose. We can use this to create the herbs in Romaine and thereby impart actual knowledge onto the player bolstering the Educational Entertainment perspective of our games.

'Blighted' herbs also have altered effects.

Cards can be combined to create new effects, for example let's say the player chose the Druid starting deck which has a foraging sickle in it. While they are out foraging they come across some deadly nightshade {Belladonna} and add this card to the deck. During a combat encounter they can combine the nightshade and the sickle to have an effect applied either for that turn or the entire round.

Here is an example of what I think the player's experience could be like in a single playthrough:
	NPC knocks on the door and enters the shop approaching the player at their workbench. 
	"My son was out playing out by the old mill on the south side of town and has come down with a relentless fever, can you help him please?"
	The player accepts the request and begins their journey into the forest. This is treated a bit like a node map but the player instead has a hex-map with them that only reveals the hexes they have already been to in that run. 
	This particular forest shifts and changes procedurally with every new run based on a factors such as weather, time of day, the herbs they are after etc.
